Privacy Policy for Pocket Swara

Last Updated: April 12, 2026

Overview

Pocket Swara ("the App") is an Indian classical music practice tool. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The App operates entirely on your device. No user accounts are required, and no data is sent to external servers.

Microphone Access

The App may request access to your device's microphone. Microphone audio is processed entirely on your device in real time for pitch detection purposes. Audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Data Storage

All App settings (such as your selected base pitch and raga preferences) are stored locally on your device. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App contains no ads.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at pocketswara@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No data collection
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No ads
  • No user accounts
  • No network requests
  • All processing happens on your device
